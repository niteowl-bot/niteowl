# Before changing Remy's behaviour

Voice, booking, timezone, calendar, lead-capture and auth behaviour is governed by standing
rules in **`docs/REMY_BEHAVIOUR_RULES.md`**. Each one exists to stop a specific defect
returning. **Read the rule before changing the behaviour it governs.**
`PROJECT_CONTEXT.md` indexes them and holds current state; `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` is the
architecture canon; `CHANGELOG.md` is the shipped history.
